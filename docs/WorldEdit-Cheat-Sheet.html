WorldEdit Cheat Sheet

Paper 26.3 + WorldEdit 7.4.6. Updated Oct 20, 2026. Press Ctrl+F to search.

Ground rules

  • Two slashes (//) for commands that change the world: //brush, //set, //replace, //gmask, //undo.
  • One slash (/) for settings on the brush you're holding: /mask, /size, /mat, /range, /none.
  • Brush first, then mask. Re-running a //brush line makes a new brush and wipes its mask. Change size with /size 12 instead of retyping the brush.
  • Type each command on its own line. Pasting a brush and its mask together makes WorldEdit reject them.
  • Masks belong to the item you're holding. Put each brush on its own item. /mask alone clears it.
  • Global mask (//gmask ...) affects every tool, including paste. Always turn it off afterward with //gmask.
  • Undo / redo: //undo, //redo. Wand: //wand, then left-click and right-click two corners.
  • Brush size limit is 100 (raised in WorldEdit's config). After config edits, run /worldedit reload.

Masks

Mask Effect
/mask air Only fills empty space; never replaces anything
/mask stone Only changes stone
/mask grass_block,dirt Several blocks: comma, no spaces
/mask !stone Everything except stone
/mask >grass_block Only the block sitting on top of grass
/mask =y>67 Only above Y 67 (=y<67 below, =y>=60&&y<=80 between)
//gmask air + a brush /mask Both apply together (AND)
  • Placing plants on top: use /mask >grass_block,podzol,... but never list dirt, because grass sits on dirt and the brush hits the grass. If the ground is several layers of the same block, also run //gmask air.
  • Two conditions at once: /mask takes only one. Put the second in //gmask, for example //gmask =y>64 plus /mask stone.

Selections and shapes

Command What it does
//pos1 2366,50,914 Set a corner by coordinates (//pos2 the same way)
//hpos1, //hpos2 Select the block you're looking at
//expand 3 right Grow the selection (left, up, down, forward, back)
//set sand Fill the selection
//replace sand stone Swap one block for another in the selection
//replace !stone sand Replace everything except stone
//replace cherry_leaves 85%cherry_leaves,15%flowering_azalea_leaves Replace a percentage
//walls stone_bricks Only the outer walls of the selection
//sphere sand 5 Sphere around you (//sphere sand 5 true sits on the ground)
//hsphere sand 5 Hollow sphere
//cyl cherry_wood 5 10 Cylinder, radius 5, 10 tall (//hcyl hollow)
//fill dirt 10 Fill a hole below you (//fillr includes caves)
//copy -m bone_block Copy only certain blocks
//paste -a Paste without the copy's air erasing things
//rotate 180, //flip down Turn or flip the clipboard
//schem save name, //schem load name Save and reload a copied build

Exact box (4 wide, 5 long, 10 tall): stand at a corner, then //pos1, //pos2, //expand 3 right, //expand 4 forward, //expand 9 up, //set stone. Each expand is one less than the size.

3×3 hole, 1 deep: look at the center block, //hpos1, //hpos2, //expand 1 left, //expand 1 right, //expand 1 up, //expand 1 down, //set air.

Brushes

Brush Usual mask What it does
//brush sphere sand 5 /mask air Dome on the ground, keeps what's under it
//brush cylinder cherry_wood 5 10 /mask air Pillar built up from the clicked block
//brush cylinder air 3 -5 none Straight-walled pit going down
//brush apply cuboid 2 set sand optional 5×5×5 cube (1 = 3 wide, 3 = 7 wide)
//brush clipboard -a -o none Pastes your clipboard where you click
//brush smooth 5 4 none Smooths terrain (radius, passes)
//brush erode 3 / //brush dilate 3 none Knock off bumps / fill dents, any direction
//brush deform sphere 5 y+=3 none Lowers terrain 3 per click
//brush deform sphere 5 y-=3 none Raises terrain 3 per click
//brush sphere air 3 none Digs a round hole
//brush sphere 70%andesite,30%stone 4 /mask stone Rock pockets inside a mountain

Any exact shape as a brush: build it once, //copy, //set air, then //brush clipboard -a -o on an item.

Smooth an underside: select it, //copy, //flip down, //paste, smooth, then flip back.

Caves

  1. Carve: //brush sphere air 4, changing /size between 3 and 7.
  2. Roughen: alternate //brush erode 3 and //brush dilate 3.
  3. Texture walls: //brush sphere 50%stone,20%andesite,15%tuff,10%cobblestone,5%mossy_cobblestone 4 with /mask stone.
  4. Details by hand: dripstone, glow lichen, cave vines with glow berries.

Water and rivers

  1. Carve a U channel: //brush cylinder air 3 -5, clicking along the ground surface.
  2. Flat bottom (optional): //gmask =y>=60 at your riverbed height, then //gmask to clear.
  3. Round the banks: //brush smooth 3 2 on the top edges.
  4. Riverbed: clear the gmask first, then //brush sphere 50%sand,30%gravel,20%clay 3 with /mask dirt,grass_block,coarse_dirt,stone,andesite,diorite,granite,tuff. Swampy: 40%mud,30%clay,30%gravel. Rocky stream: 50%gravel,30%cobblestone,20%mossy_cobblestone.

  5. Fill: stand at the water height and run //fill water 15. Or brush: //gmask =y<=63, //brush sphere water 5, /mask air.

  6. One shallow layer on the bed: //gmask air, //brush sphere water 5, /mask >sand,gravel,clay.

  7. Flat single layer at one height: //gmask =y==62 and the water brush with /mask air.
  8. Freeze a pond: select it, //replace water ice.

Trees and plants

Forest brush: //brush forest cylinder 8 3 dark_oak (radius 8, 3% density). Use Minecraft's names with underscores, and never names starting with trees_ (they crash).

Tree name Look
oak, fancy_oak Plain oak / big sprawling oak
dark_oak Thick, bushy canopy
azalea_tree Small tree with pink and green flowers
jungle_bush Leafy shrubs for gaps
swamp_oak Oak with hanging vines
jungle_tree, mega_jungle_tree Tall and lush
cherry, birch, mangrove, spruce Other options
  • Leaves on branches: //brush sphere cherry_leaves[persistent=true] 3 with /mask air. [persistent=true] stops decay.
  • Square shrubs: //brush apply cuboid 1 set spruce_leaves[persistent=true] with /mask air.
  • Round shrubs: //brush sphere spruce_leaves[persistent=true] 1 with /mask air.
  • Scattered leaf cubes: //brush sphere 92%air,8%spruce_leaves[persistent=true] 6 with /mask >grass_block,podzol,coarse_dirt,moss_block,snow_block.
  • Protect existing bushes from a plant brush: //gmask !spruce_leaves.
  • Quick flowers in a selection: //flora 10.

Ground cover (meadow): //brush sphere 50%air,18%short_grass,10%fern,6%bush,6%moss_carpet,4%azalea,2%flowering_azalea,2%firefly_bush,2%lily_of_the_valley 5 with /mask >grass_block,moss_block,podzol

Flower patches: //brush sphere 40%air,20%wildflowers,15%pink_petals,8%azure_bluet,7%oxeye_daisy,5%cornflower,5%allium 3 with /mask >grass_block,moss_block,podzol

Spruce forest floor: first //brush sphere 50%podzol,30%grass_block,15%coarse_dirt,5%moss_block 5 with /mask grass_block, then plants //brush sphere 55%air,20%fern,12%short_grass,5%sweet_berry_bush[age=3],4%brown_mushroom,2%red_mushroom,2%moss_carpet 5 with /mask >grass_block,podzol,coarse_dirt,moss_block

Two-block plants (tall_grass, large_fern) only place their bottom half from a brush; add them by hand.

Clearing

All use //brush sphere air 8, then the mask. Never add grass_block.

  • Oak and birch forest: /mask oak_log,oak_wood,oak_leaves,birch_log,birch_wood,birch_leaves,leaf_litter,short_grass,tall_grass,vine,bee_nest
  • Spruce forest: /mask spruce_log,spruce_wood,spruce_leaves,short_grass,tall_grass,fern,large_fern,sweet_berry_bush,brown_mushroom,red_mushroom,leaf_litter
  • Big cube of air: //brush apply cuboid 4 set air (9×9×9)
  • Dropped items: /kill @e[type=item]

Zone palettes

Ground brushes use /mask grass_block. Wall brushes use /mask stone. Detail brushes use the > mask shown.

Life (lush moss)

  • Ground edge: //brush sphere 70%grass_block,25%moss_block,5%rooted_dirt 5
  • Ground center: //brush sphere 50%moss_block,35%grass_block,15%rooted_dirt 5 (deeper green: add 10%green_concrete_powder)
  • Mossy cliffs: //brush sphere 40%moss_block,30%mossy_cobblestone,30%stone 4
  • Plants: //brush sphere 60%air,15%moss_carpet,8%fern,6%short_grass,5%azalea,3%flowering_azalea,3%pink_petals 5 with /mask >grass_block,moss_block
  • Light: glow berries, shroomlight, invisible light block. Biome: dark_forest or jungle.

Cherry grove

  • Logs: //replace cherry_wood 73%cherry_wood,15%mangrove_wood,10%dark_oak_wood,2%shroomlight
  • Canopy lights: //replace cherry_leaves 95%cherry_leaves[persistent=true],5%pearlescent_froglight
  • Ground: pink petals; lanterns on chains.

Enchanted meadow

  • Ground: //brush sphere 55%grass_block,20%podzol,15%moss_block,10%coarse_dirt 5
  • Plants: //brush sphere 55%air,15%short_grass,8%fern,6%wildflowers,4%oxeye_daisy,4%azure_bluet,3%allium,3%lily_of_the_valley,2%firefly_bush 5 with /mask >grass_block,podzol,moss_block
  • Biome: meadow or flower_forest.

Winter

  • Snowfall: //snow 10 (undo with //thaw 10)
  • Ground: //brush sphere 60%snow_block,20%grass_block[snowy=true],12%packed_ice,8%blue_ice 5
  • Snow drifts: //brush sphere 50%snow,30%snow[layers=2],15%snow[layers=3],5%snow[layers=4] 5 with /mask >grass_block,snow_block
  • Icy rock: //brush sphere 40%packed_ice,35%blue_ice,25%ice 4
  • Plants: //brush sphere 75%air,8%fern,7%short_grass,5%dead_bush,3%sweet_berry_bush,2%lily_of_the_valley 4 with /mask >grass_block,snow_block
  • Biome: snowy_plains, snowy_taiga, grove. Avoid powder_snow.

Swift (wind and sky)

  • Spires: //cyl 50%calcite,30%diorite,20%smooth_stone 3 25
  • Cliffs: //brush sphere 45%calcite,30%diorite,15%smooth_stone,10%tuff 4
  • Plants: //brush sphere 60%air,25%short_grass,10%azure_bluet,5%oxeye_daisy 5 with /mask >grass_block,coarse_dirt
  • Biome: stony_peaks or windswept_hills. Birch trees, arches, floating islands.

Tank (rugged fortress)

  • Ground: //brush sphere 35%coarse_dirt,25%packed_mud,15%gravel,10%cobblestone,10%andesite,5%rooted_dirt 5
  • Cliffs: //brush sphere 35%deepslate,25%cobbled_deepslate,20%tuff,15%andesite,5%cobblestone 4
  • Built walls: //walls 40%stone_bricks,25%cracked_stone_bricks,20%mossy_stone_bricks,15%andesite
  • Plants: //brush sphere 85%air,8%short_grass,4%dead_bush,3%fern 5 with /mask >coarse_dirt,packed_mud,gravel,grass_block
  • Biome: windswept_gravelly_hills.

Fire (volcanic)

  • Ground: //brush sphere 35%blackstone,25%basalt,15%coarse_dirt,10%red_sand,10%netherrack,5%magma_block 5 (magma burns; keep it off paths)
  • Cliffs: //brush sphere 30%blackstone,20%basalt,20%orange_terracotta,15%red_terracotta,10%terracotta,5%magma_block 4
  • Built walls: //walls 40%polished_blackstone_bricks,25%cracked_polished_blackstone_bricks,20%nether_bricks,15%red_nether_bricks
  • Details: //brush sphere 88%air,6%dead_bush,4%crimson_roots,2%fire 5 with /mask >blackstone,basalt,coarse_dirt,red_sand,netherrack (plus //gmask air)
  • Biome: myserver:fire_zone (custom orange sky).

Death

  • Ground: //brush sphere 30%soul_soil,20%coarse_dirt,15%gray_concrete_powder,15%podzol,10%soul_sand,10%gravel 5 with /mask grass_block only (not dirt)
  • Cliffs: //brush sphere 35%deepslate,25%blackstone,15%tuff,15%cobbled_deepslate,10%basalt 4
  • Built walls: //walls 35%cracked_deepslate_bricks,25%deepslate_bricks,20%polished_blackstone_bricks,10%cracked_polished_blackstone_bricks,10%deepslate_tiles
  • Details: //brush sphere 88%air,6%dead_bush,3%short_grass,2%cobweb,1%soul_fire 5, then /mask >soul_soil,coarse_dirt,podzol,gravel,grass_block, then //gmask air
  • Light: soul lanterns, soul torches, crying obsidian. Wither roses only where players can't walk; no sculk shriekers.
  • Biome: myserver:death_zone (black sky, dark purple water).

Biomes and custom skies

  • Biome brush: //brush biome -c sphere 7 <biome>. Selection: //setbiome <biome>. Rejoin to see changes.
  • Mountain biomes: stony_peaks, windswept_hills, meadow, jagged_peaks, frozen_peaks, grove, snowy_slopes.
  • Custom (datapack zone_skies): myserver:fire_zone has an orange-red sky, red haze, ash, and orange water. myserver:death_zone has a black sky, dark purple water, ash, and dead grass. Both have no mob spawns.
  • Datapack location: world\datapacks\zone_skies. New biomes need a server restart.
  • Find biomes: /locate biome minecraft:cherry_grove, or your seed (/seed) in Chunkbase Biome Finder.
  • Biomes snap to 4-block chunks. Lower Options → Video Settings → Biome Blend for sharper edges.

Structure templates

Build once, save it, and paste it around. Make 2–3 variants of each piece.

  1. Save: select the build, stand where it should line up, then //copy (or //copy -m bone_block,... for specific blocks) and //schem save name.
  2. Place: //schem load name, then //rotate 90 if needed, then //paste -a. Sink some a block or two lower so they look buried.
Piece Size Idea
Gravestone Small Stone/deepslate slab and wall, cracked, mossy
Soul lantern post Small Fence or chain with a hanging soul lantern
Dead tree Small Stripped dark oak or pale oak, no leaves, cobwebs
Bone arch Medium Already built
Ribcage Medium Half-buried curved rows of bone blocks
Giant skull Medium Bone blocks with soul-fire eyes, half sunk
Ruined pillar Medium Broken cracked deepslate brick columns
Crypt entrance Large Dark doorway into a hill, stairs down
